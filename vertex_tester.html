Vertex tester

A point x ∈ P is a vertex if some vector c makes x the unique minimiser of cTy over P. Drag the tip of c: the minimisers of cTy are the points of P furthest in the direction −c. Can you find a c for every corner of the running example?

Drag the orange arrow tip, or select it and use the arrow keys (hold Shift for small steps). With snapping on, the tip jumps to multiples of ½ when it gets close, which makes ties between two corners easy to hit. The faint lines are level lines cTy = constant; the bold one is the lowest level that still touches P. To zoom, use the buttons or hold Ctrl (⌘ on a Mac) and scroll; drag the background to pan. On a touch screen, use two fingers to pan and pinch to zoom.
