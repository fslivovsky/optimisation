Slope and curvature in a direction

Cut the graph of f along a line through x in direction d. The cut is the one-variable function g(t) = f(x + t d). Its slope at t = 0 is g′(0) = ∇f(x)Td, given by the gradient, and its curvature is g″(0) = dT∇²f(x)d, given by the Hessian. Drag the point and the tip of the direction arrow in the middle view.
