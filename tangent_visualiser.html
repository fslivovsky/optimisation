Derivatives, gradients and tangents

Drag the point along the curve. The green line is the tangent, with slope f′(x). Zoom with Ctrl or ⌘ and the mouse wheel.

For a convex function the tangent lies below the whole graph. Switch to a function that is not convex and look for points where the tangent cuts the graph. With the secant switched on, shrink h and watch the secant turn into the tangent.

Level curves. Drag the point. Red: ∇f, green: −∇f. Ctrl or ⌘ and the wheel to zoom.

The graph with its tangent plane. Drag to rotate, Ctrl or ⌘ and the wheel to zoom.

The tangent plane is f(x) + ∇f(x)T(y − x). For a convex function it lies below the whole graph. The gradient is perpendicular to the level curve through the point.
