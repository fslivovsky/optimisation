Convex function visualiser

f is convex if f(θx + (1 − θ)y) ≤ θf(x) + (1 − θ)f(y) for all x, y in its (convex) domain and all θ ∈ [0, 1]: the graph lies below every chord.

Drag the points on the graph or on the axis, or use the sliders. To zoom, use the buttons or hold Ctrl (⌘ on a Mac) and scroll; drag the background to pan. On a touch screen, use two fingers to pan and pinch to zoom. Your own function can use + − * / ^, sin, cos, tan, exp, log, sqrt, abs, max, min, pi and e.
