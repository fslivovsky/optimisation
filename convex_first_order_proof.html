Tangent inequality ⇒ convexity

If f(w) ≥ f(z) + ∇f(z)T(w − z) for all w, z, then f is convex. Take z = θx + (1 − θ)y and apply the inequality at z with w = x and w = y.

Green gaps: the two inequalities, f(x) ≥ ℓ(x) and f(y) ≥ ℓ(y).

Tangent ℓ is affine, so θℓ(x) + (1 − θ)ℓ(y) = ℓ(z) = f(z): the gradient terms cancel because θ(x − z) + (1 − θ)(y − z) = 0.

Adding the gaps with weights θ, 1 − θ lifts f(z) to the chord: θf(x) + (1 − θ)f(y) ≥ f(z).

Nothing here uses that the tangent varies continuously with z: one supporting line at the single point z is all the argument needs.
