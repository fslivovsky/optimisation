Slack meter

In standard form, every inequality gets a slack variable: aTx ≤ b becomes aTx + s = b with s ≥ 0. The slack measures how far the point is from that constraint's boundary. It is zero exactly when the constraint is active, and negative when the constraint is violated.

Drag the point, or click it and use the arrow keys (hold Shift for small steps). With snapping on, the point jumps onto a constraint line or corner when it gets close, so slacks become exactly zero. You may drag the point outside the polyhedron to see what a negative slack means.
