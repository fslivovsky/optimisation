Matrix inverse trainer

To invert a square matrix B, write it next to the identity as [B | I] and apply row operations until the left block is I. The right block is then B−1. Work one column at a time: bring a nonzero entry to the diagonal (the pivot), scale its row so the pivot is 1, then clear every other entry in that column. Answers may be fractions such as −3/2.

Current [B | I]

Allowed row operations: swap two rows, multiply a row by a nonzero number, subtract a multiple of one row from another. 3 × 3, may be singular: about one problem in three has no inverse. Running example bases: B consists of columns of the standard form of the running example, as in Simplex.
