L-smoothness and the descent lemma

If the curvature of f is at most L everywhere, then f lies below the parabola f(x) + f′(x)(y − x) + (L/2)(y − x)². Its minimum is at y = x − f′(x)/L, the gradient step with t = 1/L, which is therefore guaranteed to lower f. Drag the point, and change L to see when the guarantee holds.
